# Calibration Plan

Calibration is an optional consistency exercise before a longer rating session. It is not a source of prefilled ratings and does not establish correctness by itself.

1. Select a small set of permitted outputs covering the relevant module. Do not choose examples because they are known to contain a particular flaw.
2. Read each prompt first and review the applicable rubric anchors.
3. Rate each output independently. Record criterion-level evidence and uncertainty in a separate dated log.
4. Pause, then revisit a subset without looking at the earlier scores.
5. Compare your entries. Where ratings differ, identify whether the change came from missed evidence, an unclear anchor, fatigue, or genuine ambiguity.
6. If needed, clarify your interpretation of an anchor in a dated note. Do not change historical scores silently.
7. Decide whether the rubric is usable for your purposes. Record unresolved issues.

There is no target score or required agreement threshold. A self-comparison is not inter-rater reliability or proof of validity. Calibration was not conducted for the current review.
