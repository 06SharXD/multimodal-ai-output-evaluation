# Video Prompt Set

These prompts specify short clips. Use only permitted tools/materials and record actual duration, tool, version, and settings in the asset manifest. No outputs or observations are included.

| Prompt ID | Prompt |
|---|---|
| VID-01 | A person in a yellow raincoat walks from left to right along a wet sidewalk while holding a closed black umbrella. A light breeze moves a few leaves. Keep the raincoat and umbrella consistent throughout a 6-second clip. |
| VID-02 | A red paper kite rises slowly above a grassy hill on a breezy afternoon. The camera remains stationary; the kite string stays attached and visible. Create a 6-second clip with continuous, gentle motion. |
| VID-03 | A small white sailboat moves steadily from left to right across a calm lake at sunset. Keep the boat and sail consistent. Use a stationary wide shot lasting 6 seconds. |
| VID-04 | A baker places one round loaf on a wooden cooling rack, then withdraws both hands. The loaf remains on the rack. Show the full action in a steady 6-second medium shot. |
| VID-05 | A city tram travels forward along a straight track through a tree-lined street in daylight. Keep the tram's appearance and direction consistent. Use a 6-second side view with no cuts. |
| VID-06 | A single green leaf drifts down from a branch and lands on a still pond, making small ripples. Keep the leaf's color and shape consistent. Use a 6-second close shot. |

Review the whole clip and, where useful, inspect timestamps or frames. The prompt is not evidence of a flaw; document only personally observed evidence.
