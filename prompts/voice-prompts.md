# Voice and Conversational Audio Prompt Set

These short scripts support human review of speech delivery. No generated audio or evaluations are included. Use an authorized voice tool and record voice/model/version and settings.

## Read-aloud tasks

| Prompt ID | Spoken text | Task |
|---|---|---|
| VOX-01 | “The package will arrive on Thursday, between two and four in the afternoon.” | Read in a clear, neutral customer-service tone. |
| VOX-02 | “Please place the blue folder beside the window, then wait for the next instruction.” | Read at a natural pace with clear wording. |
| VOX-03 | “In 2024, the museum welcomed 18,450 visitors from 37 countries.” | Read clearly, including the numbers. |
| VOX-04 | “We can reschedule the meeting for Tuesday, May 14, or Friday, May 17.” | Read with clear dates and a helpful, neutral tone. |

## Conversational response tasks

Use each scenario as the prompt to the voice model and review the spoken response against that request. The task descriptions are not answer keys.

| Prompt ID | User request |
|---|---|
| CONV-01 | “I have ten minutes before my bus. Suggest a quick way to organize the notes I need for class.” |
| CONV-02 | “I missed the first step in your instructions. Could you repeat it more slowly?” |
| CONV-03 | “I’m choosing between a short walk and stretching indoors. Help me decide based on rainy weather.” |
| CONV-04 | “Can you explain the difference between a calendar reminder and a task list in two simple sentences?” |

Record the original request, output identity, playback conditions, and your own observations. Check model-generated claims against the audio itself.
