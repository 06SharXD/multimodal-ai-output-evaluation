# Multimodal AI Output Evaluation — Results Report

## Summary

The project owner generated and personally reviewed six image outputs, six video outputs, and four read-aloud audio outputs against the repository prompts. The owner supplied one holistic overall score and a qualitative observation for each output. This is a small, single-reviewer exercise and does not establish general model performance.

## Scope and materials

The outputs were generated with ChatGPT image generation, Gemini video generation, and ElevenLabs voice generation. See the [asset manifest](../data/asset-manifest.csv) and [methodology](methodology.md) for model and settings details. The media files are available in `data/assets/images/`, `data/assets/videos/`, and `data/assets/audio/`.

## Method

The owner reviewed each output and supplied the judgments in [owner-ratings.csv](../ratings/owner-ratings.csv). Each score is a holistic 1–5 overall impression, not a score for an individual rubric criterion. The modality rubrics were not applied criterion by criterion. Audio was reviewed in a quiet place at comfortable volume using Apple EarPods.

## Results

| Modality | Outputs rated | Overall score distribution |
|---|---:|---|
| Images | 6 | 1: one output; 2: three; 5: two |
| Videos | 6 | 2: one output; 3: four; 4: one |
| Read-aloud audio | 4 | 5: four outputs |

The distribution summarizes the owner's judgments for these outputs. It is not a comparison of services or a claim about general quality.

## Owner-provided observations

### Images

- **IMG-01 (5):** The owner felt it looked realistic, with the bicycle as the main focus and good quality.
- **IMG-02 (5):** The owner praised the polished, DSLR-like appearance, shadows, and details in the cloth, cup, lemon slices, and leaves.
- **IMG-03 (1):** The owner disliked the animated/cartoonized style and felt it looked AI-generated at first glance.
- **IMG-04 (2):** The watercolor style matched the prompt, but the owner still felt it looked AI-generated and was unsure why; they also described it as good.
- **IMG-05 (2):** The notebook looked artificial to the owner; lighting and shadows were positives.
- **IMG-06 (2):** The owner felt it could look like a Canva design and thought some viewers might identify it as AI-generated.

### Videos

- **VID-01 (4):** Footstep and water audio matched well. The owner noticed possible distortion in the middle, was unsure whether it was wind or an intended effect, and noted a watermark.
- **VID-02 (3):** The owner noted the same unspecified audio issue as VID-01 and a watermark; the video did not look AI-generated to them.
- **VID-03 (3):** The owner reported an audio issue and lower video quality; no watermark was noticed.
- **VID-04 (2):** The owner felt it looked AI-generated and noted a watermark; video quality was fine.
- **VID-05 (3):** The owner felt it looked realistic and noted no watermark, but reported an audio issue.
- **VID-06 (3):** The owner liked the video overall but noticed an abrupt change to the water background when the leaf reached the water, and noted a watermark.

### Read-aloud audio

The owner gave **VOX-01 through VOX-04 a score of 5** and reported no defects. They noted that the voice slowed around numbers and held slightly around dates or times, helping emphasize those details.

## Comparisons

No pairwise comparisons or winner selections were recorded.

## Limitations

This is one reviewer's holistic assessment of 16 outputs. Criterion-level rubric scores, confidence values, exact evidence timestamps for most video/audio notes, review-session dates, session numbers, and time spent were not recorded. Some generation details are unknown. The media files are unavailable in the public repository for independent inspection. Whether a second “Quiet Morning” poster output exists remains unconfirmed.

## Conclusion and next steps

These records support a limited statement: the owner generated and personally reviewed these outputs and documented overall impressions and observations. They do not support conclusions about general model performance or a rubric-by-rubric comparison.

Upload the media files through GitHub if public sharing remains permitted. Add review-session details if they can be recalled. A criterion-by-criterion rubric pass is optional; if performed, record evidence and scores separately.
