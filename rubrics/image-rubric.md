# Image Evaluation Rubric

Read the prompt before viewing the image. Rate adherence and quality separately. Use the general scale in scale-and-recording-guide.md; scores 2 and 4 are intermediate. Record evidence locations such as image regions.

| Criterion | 1 anchor | 3 anchor | 5 anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prompt adherence | Major requested content or constraints are absent or contradicted. | Main request is partly met; meaningful detail is missing or altered. | Requested content and constraints are clearly met. |
| Object and scene correctness | Key objects, attributes, or relationships are substantially incorrect. | Main objects are recognizable but some attributes or relations are unclear. | Objects, attributes, and spatial relationships match the prompt. |
| Completeness and framing | Important requested content is missing or cut off. | Most content is present; framing or omissions limit part of the request. | Requested elements are present and framed as requested. |
| Visual coherence | Major structural inconsistencies make the scene difficult to interpret. | Scene is broadly coherent with noticeable localized inconsistencies. | Scene is coherent and internally consistent. |
| Text rendering | Requested text is missing, unreadable, or materially incorrect. | Text is partly legible or has a noticeable minor discrepancy. | Requested text is legible and matches the prompt. |
| Presentation quality | Technical or stylistic limitations substantially obstruct intended use. | Usable overall with noticeable limitations. | Presentation is clear and suitable for the requested style. |

Text rendering may be marked not applicable where no text is requested. Do not assume a category is flawed; note only evidence you observe. Describe any anatomy or object-form issue by location and visible characteristics rather than a generic label.
