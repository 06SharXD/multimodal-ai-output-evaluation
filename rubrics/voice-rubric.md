# Voice and Conversational Audio Evaluation Rubric

Read the task prompt before listening. Use a quiet environment, comfortable consistent volume, and note playback conditions. Separate content adherence from delivery quality.

| Criterion | 1 anchor | 3 anchor | 5 anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prompt adherence | Response materially misses the task, content, or constraints. | Core request is addressed but meaningful content or constraints are missed. | Response addresses the request and follows its constraints. |
| Naturalness | Delivery is difficult to follow or consistently poorly matched to conversational speech. | Generally understandable with noticeable unnatural delivery in places. | Delivery sounds natural and easy to follow for the task. |
| Pronunciation and clarity | Words are frequently unclear or pronounced in a way that obstructs understanding. | Mostly clear with localized pronunciation or articulation issues. | Words are clear and readily understandable. |
| Pauses, rhythm, and intonation | Timing or intonation substantially impairs comprehension or task fit. | Understandable but with noticeable pacing or prosody limitations. | Pace, pauses, rhythm, and intonation fit the task. |
| Conversational appropriateness | Tone is materially mismatched to the request or context. | Tone is broadly suitable with noticeable mismatch in places. | Tone is suitable and responsive to the request. |
| Audio intelligibility | Noise, clipping, or level issues substantially obstruct listening. | Speech is intelligible but has noticeable technical limitations. | Speech is clear at a comfortable level without material obstruction. |

Record a timestamp or phrase for specific delivery observations. Do not infer a voice trait from expectations about the model. Mark a criterion not applicable only when the task genuinely does not call for it.
