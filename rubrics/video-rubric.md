# Video Evaluation Rubric

Read the prompt first. Review the whole clip, then use timestamps or frame ranges for evidence. Score prompt adherence separately from temporal and visual quality.

| Criterion | 1 anchor | 3 anchor | 5 anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prompt adherence | Main action, entities, or constraints materially contradict the prompt. | Main idea is present but meaningful details or constraints are missed. | Requested action, entities, and constraints are clearly represented. |
| Temporal consistency | Frequent or severe changes make continuity difficult to follow. | Some inconsistencies occur but the action remains understandable. | Entities and scene remain consistent across the clip. |
| Motion naturalness | Motion is substantially discontinuous, implausible, or difficult to follow. | Motion is understandable with noticeable irregularities. | Motion appears continuous and appropriate to the requested action. |
| Action and spatial continuity | The action or object path is unclear or repeatedly breaks. | The action is mostly trackable with a noticeable discontinuity. | The action progresses clearly and spatially consistently. |
| Frame-level visual quality | Repeated visual limitations materially obstruct viewing. | Usable overall with noticeable localized limitations. | Frames are clear and suitable for the requested clip. |
| Camera and scene stability | Unrequested camera/scene changes repeatedly disrupt the clip. | Some instability or unrequested changes are noticeable. | Camera and scene presentation support the requested clip. |

Record timestamps or frame ranges for specific observations. Do not describe flicker, morphing, or unnatural motion unless you personally observe it.
