# Scale and Recording Guide

Use a 1–5 scale for each criterion; scores 2 and 4 are intermediate judgments between adjacent anchors.

| Score | General anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | Major mismatch or limitation materially prevents the requested outcome. |
| 2 | Noticeable mismatch or limitation; some requested value remains. |
| 3 | Mixed or adequate result; important aspects are met and some are limited. |
| 4 | Strong result with only minor limitations. |
| 5 | Fully or very strongly meets the criterion with no material limitation observed. |

Use “uncertain” when evidence is insufficient for a confident judgment; use “not applicable” only when the criterion does not apply. Neither is numeric. Do not coerce them to 0 or include them in numeric averages.

For each scored row, include prompt ID, output ID, module, criterion, score, confidence, and an evidence location. Add an observation and rationale in your own words. Keep observation (what is seen/heard) separate from interpretation (why it matters).
